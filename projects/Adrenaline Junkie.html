Overview

'Adrenaline Junkie' is a fast-paced first-person shooter made in Unreal Engine 5 as a part of a 30+ developer team during the 'Collaborative' module of my second university year. The goal of the game is to reach the end of the level whilst navigating across a level full of dangerous traps and enemies.

Shooting
